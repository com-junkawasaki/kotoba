# ADR — Independent technical review boundary

- Status: **Accepted**
- Date: 2026-08-27
- Scope: public review material for `kotoba-lang/kotoba`

## Context

Kotoba needs independent technical evaluation of its language, capability,
package-admission, and host boundaries. Project-authored documentation can make
claims reproducible, but cannot become independent merely because another site
hosts, quotes, or reformats it. Paying for, writing, approving, or conditioning
coverage would undermine the independence the evaluation is intended to add.

## Decision

1. Publish a fixed-commit review kit containing falsifiable questions, positive
   and negative cases, known boundaries, and raw-evidence guidance.
2. Reviewers choose their own methods, conclusions, wording, and publication.
3. The project does not pay for coverage, ghostwrite, exchange coverage,
   request backlinks, or require favorable findings.
4. Factual clarification is allowed; project pre-publication approval is not.
5. Solicitation, material support, unusual access, and reviewer relationships
   must be disclosed.
6. Founder articles, guest posts, interviews, announcements, and
   project-authored papers remain affiliated primary material.
7. Negative, mixed, and non-reproducible results remain valid outcomes.

## Consequences

The process may produce criticism or no publication. That is preferable to a
controlled source presented as independent. This kit improves reproducibility;
it does not certify security, establish production readiness, or itself satisfy
an external notability requirement.
